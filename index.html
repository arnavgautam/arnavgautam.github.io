Welcome

I'm an energy systems researcher, power system modeler, and Senior Consultant at Current Energy Group. My work operates at the intersection of power systems engineering, mathematical optimization, and energy policy to address pressing challenges in grid decarbonization, climate resilience, and technology integration.

I hold a Ph.D. in Engineering and Public Policy from Carnegie Mellon University, where my research focused on electrical distribution grid optimization, multi-criteria decision analysis, and low-carbon grid planning.

Current Focus & Recent Work

  • Distribution Resilience & Outage Risk Modeling: Developing probabilistic risk frameworks and lifecycle benefit-cost models using feeder-level outage data, OpenDSS/CYME workflows, and EPRI ClimateREADi guidance to evaluate grid modernization portfolios (e.g., Green Mountain Power's Zero Outages Initiative).

  • Advanced DER Orchestration & Grid-Enhancing Technologies (GETs): Researching operational architectures for FAST-DERMS, flexible interconnection, voltage optimization, situational awareness, and automated FLISR capabilities to enable high-DER utility networks.

  • Large-Load & Data Center Grid Integration: Analyzing flexible interconnection frameworks, co-located generation, and tariff structures to accommodate rapid growth in data center demand within distribution and sub-transmission constraints.

  • Integrated System Planning: Supporting regulatory entities in facilitating DER adoption, non-wires alternatives, and resilience metrics within state electric system planning frameworks.
